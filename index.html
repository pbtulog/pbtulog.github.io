Maximize profit before time runs out.

Six orders need to move from Hannover or Ludwigshafen to Verona. You can send each one directly, or via the shared hub in München — but capacities are all limited.

  • Tap an 📦order, then tap a highlighted connection on the 🗺️map to pick a wagon.
  • Every leg costs money to run, and changing trains at München costs an extra transshipment fee.
  • Any order you never fully route costs you a fixed rejection penalty at the end.
  • Goal: maximize profits (revenue minus costs).
